# Legal Document Management — Data Governance Vocabulary

---

## Privacy
*Mutually exclusive*

### Contains Personal Data
Document includes information that identifies or relates to a natural person — full GDPR obligations apply.

> Counterparty identification sections, signatory details, employment contracts containing personal circumstances, and court filings naming individuals.

### Client Confidential — No Personal Data
Document contains commercially or professionally sensitive information about a client organisation but no personal data.

> Corporate merger terms, non-disclosure agreements between entities, and transaction documents where all parties are legal persons with no individual identifiers.

### Commercially Sensitive — Internal
Document contains sensitive internal business information — financial projections, strategic advice, or pricing — but no personal or client-confidential content.

> Internal legal opinion on a prospective acquisition, fee estimate worksheets, and internal matter budget records.

### Non-Sensitive
Document contains no personal, client, or commercially sensitive information.

> Publicly available statutory instruments referenced in a matter, court procedural guidance notes, and published regulatory guidance used as precedent.

---

## Criticality
*Mutually exclusive*

### Binding Legal Instrument
A fully executed document that creates, modifies, or extinguishes legal rights and obligations.

> Signed contracts, executed deeds, court orders, regulatory licences, and settlement agreements — loss or corruption would impair the ability to enforce or contest legal positions.

### Evidentiary
A document whose authenticity and integrity are material to an active or anticipated legal proceeding.

> Correspondence, board minutes, and transaction records subject to disclosure obligations or relied upon as evidence in litigation or regulatory proceedings.

### Legal Advice
A document containing privileged legal opinion — protected from compelled disclosure but critical to the client's position.

> Counsel's written advice on liability exposure, litigation risk assessments, and internal memoranda capturing legal strategy.

### Reference and Precedent
A document used to inform current legal work but which creates no direct obligation or evidentiary duty.

> Precedent clause libraries, jurisdiction-specific template banks, and anonymised matter summaries used for knowledge management.

### Administrative
Operational documentation supporting matter management with no direct legal effect or evidential function.

> Matter intake forms, billing records, court filing receipts, and task tracking notes.

## Lifecycle
*Mutually exclusive*

### Draft
Document is under active preparation and has not been executed, submitted, or formally issued.

> Working versions of contracts under negotiation, pleadings in preparation, and legal opinions awaiting partner review.

### Under Review
Document has been issued for review, negotiation, or approval but has not yet reached a final executed state.

> Counterparty redlines received and under internal review; regulatory submissions awaiting agency acknowledgement.

### Executed
Document has been formally signed, filed, sealed, or otherwise completed by all required parties.

> Signed agreements, filed court documents, issued regulatory licences, and notarised instruments.

### Superseded
Document has been replaced by a later version or amendment — retained for historical reference and audit trail purposes.

> Original contracts replaced by a restated version; superseded regulatory submissions retained alongside the accepted final filing.

### Expired
Document had a defined term that has elapsed; no longer operative but retained for the legally required period.

> Lease agreements past their contractual end date, expired licences, and time-limited consent orders no longer in force.

### Destroyed
Document has been deleted or physically destroyed in accordance with the approved retention schedule.

> Records destroyed after satisfaction of all statutory and contractual retention obligations, with destruction certificate issued.

## Retention
*Mutually exclusive*

### Legal Hold
Retention suspended indefinitely pending the resolution of active litigation, investigation, or regulatory inquiry.

> All documents relevant to a notified claim or regulatory dawn raid; standard retention schedules are overridden until the hold is formally lifted.

### Statutory Mandated
Retention period and format prescribed by law, court rule, or regulatory requirement.

> Corporate records retained for the Companies Act minimum; employment documents retained per applicable employment law; court bundles retained per civil procedure rules.

### Contractual Term Plus Tail
Retained for the duration of the contract plus a defined post-expiry period specified in the agreement or firm policy.

> Transaction documents retained for the contract term plus six years to cover the standard limitation period for breach of contract claims.

### Matter Closure
Retained for a defined period from the closure of the matter, per the firm's or organisation's document retention policy.

> Client files closed on completion of an instruction; retained for the firm's standard post-closure period before destruction review.

### Transient Working
Working copies and intermediate drafts with no independent retention requirement — to be destroyed once superseded by a final version.

> Auto-save drafts, comparison redlines that have been incorporated, and temporary working copies created solely for formatting purposes.

## QualityTrust
*Mutually exclusive*

### Executed Original
The legally operative version bearing authentic signatures, seals, or electronic execution artefacts — the authoritative copy for enforcement purposes.

> Wet-ink signed counterparts, e-signed originals with a valid audit trail, and sealed court orders — these are the records on which legal rights rest.

### Certified Copy
A copy formally certified as a true and complete reproduction of the executed original by an authorised person or institution.

> Notarised copies provided to counterparties or regulators; certified extracts from court registries.

### Approved Final
A document in its final agreed text, fully reviewed and approved, but not yet in an executed state.

> A contract agreed at execution version awaiting signature; a regulatory submission approved internally before filing.

### Draft — Partner Reviewed
A draft that has been reviewed and commented on by a qualified legal reviewer but is not yet in final form.

> Marked-up documents returned from supervising partner or senior counsel with tracked changes — not yet approved for issue.

### Draft — Unreviewed
A working draft that has not yet been subject to legal review — accuracy and completeness are not assured.

> First-pass drafts, template-populated documents before review, and AI-assisted first drafts awaiting attorney review.

## ComplianceLegal
*Multi-select*

### Legal Professional Privilege
Document is protected from compelled disclosure under attorney-client privilege or legal advice privilege — access and disclosure are tightly controlled.

> Written legal advice from qualified counsel, communications made in the context of giving or seeking legal advice, and litigation strategy documents.

### Work Product Protected
Document prepared in anticipation of litigation or for trial — protected under the work product doctrine from disclosure to opposing parties.

> Internal investigation memos, litigation strategy notes, and documents created by or at the direction of counsel for use in proceedings.

### Regulatory Disclosure Obligation
Document must be disclosed to a regulator, court, or counterparty pursuant to a statutory, procedural, or contractual obligation.

> Discovery-responsive documents, mandatory regulatory filings, and contractually required reporting — withholding would constitute a breach or contempt.

### Data Protection Regulated
Document contains personal data subject to GDPR or equivalent data protection law — data subject rights and processing obligations apply.

> Contracts naming individuals, employment documentation, and court filings containing personal particulars — subject to access, rectification, and erasure rights where applicable.

### Contractually Restricted
Use or disclosure of the document is constrained by an NDA, confidentiality undertaking, or specific contractual clause.

> Confidential information received under a non-disclosure agreement; documents marked confidential under court-imposed confidentiality orders.

### Unrestricted
No privilege, protection, or regulatory constraint applies — document may be shared and used without restriction beyond standard access controls.

> Published legislation, publicly filed court judgments, and regulatory guidance documents used for reference purposes.
